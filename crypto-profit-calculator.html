Know the real result

A trade that looks profitable before fees can be much smaller after entry and exit costs. This calculator shows both gross and estimated net PnL.

How to use the crypto profit calculator

  1. Enter the amount of margin or investment you are putting into the trade.
  2. Enter leverage if you are trading a leveraged position. Leave it blank for 1×.
  3. Enter your entry and exit prices.
  4. Choose Long or Short.
  5. Optionally enter the fee percentage charged on each side.

The calculator estimates position value, gross PnL, round-trip fees, net PnL, ROI on margin and the approximate exit price needed to break even after fees.

For a long position, a price above entry produces positive gross PnL. For a short position, a price below entry produces positive gross PnL.

FAQ

Can I use this as a Bitcoin profit calculator?

Yes. Enter your Bitcoin entry and exit prices. The same basic calculation works for BTC and other linear crypto positions.

Does leverage increase my profit?

Leverage increases the position value you control for a given margin. It can therefore increase both gains and losses relative to the margin used.

Are funding fees included?

No. The calculator estimates only the trading fees you enter. Perpetual futures funding, spread, slippage and other exchange-specific costs are not included.

What does ROI mean here?

ROI here means estimated net PnL divided by the margin or investment entered into the calculator.
